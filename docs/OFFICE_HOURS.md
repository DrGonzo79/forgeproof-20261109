# Office Hours product diagnostic

## Customer

**User:** established independent jeweler producing 20–100 pieces/month. **Economic buyer:** a 20–100-member jewelry guild or juried market whose reputation depends on credible maker standards. This is intentionally narrower than “all Etsy sellers.”

## Painful status quo

The maker keeps scattered camera-roll photos and supplier emails, then creates a decorative certificate after sale. When photos are copied or a listing is flagged, there is no coherent, timestamped making record. The guild has a directory but no repeatable evidence standard.

## Desperate use case

A $500+ jewelry bestseller is copied and challenged. The maker needs to produce a coherent bench trail in minutes and give the guild or marketplace reviewer a concise sequence of materials, stages, dates and attestation. Preventing the dispute is not promised; shortening evidence assembly is the job.

## Narrowest paid wedge

Sell one **$1,500 assisted guild pilot**: 20 jewelers, 30 days, one evidence template, 100 total passports, weekly office hours and a closeout report. Do not begin with self-serve $19 plans. The pilot must prove that members complete records and that the guild will publicly stand behind the process standard.

## Observation and validation

1. Observe five jewelers make one real piece; time every capture interruption.
2. Ask them to reconstruct evidence for one previously sold piece and note missing artifacts.
3. Prototype the process with a shared form before NFC hardware.
4. Measure median capture time, completion rate, reviewer time and buyer scan rate.
5. Ask three guild directors to accept or reject the $1,500 pilot scope in writing.

Success in 30 days: ≥15 of 20 makers complete two records, median capture overhead <6 minutes/piece, reviewer time <4 minutes, ≥25% of delivered buyers scan, and the guild renews at $299/month. Stop if fewer than 10 makers complete a second record or no director accepts the paid pilot.

## Future fit

If the guild channel works, add care/repair history, ownership transfer and vertical standards for leather or ceramics. If only dispute packets are valuable, pivot to an evidence-export service. Do not expand into enterprise DPP compliance until a regulated buyer is actually pulling it.
